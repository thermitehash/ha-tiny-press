# v0.13.0 — One Spelling tile, faster AI prints, progress on tiles, lasting print counts

## Changed
- **One Spelling tile prints all four spelling practices** for the same words: unscramble, fill in the missing letters, a word search and a crossword, on one strip with one cut (the *spelling pack*, `type: spelling_pack`). It uses up to 10 unchecked words from the kid's spelling list. A longer list rotates through its words from print to print.
  - About 40 cm of paper for 10 words. It counts as one print toward kid-mode limits, like the Kid pack. The Answers button prints the key for each part.
  - The separate **Spelling search** and **Spelling crossword** buttons and card tiles are gone; their old button entities are removed on upgrade. The `button.tiny_press_spelling` button now prints the pack.
  - Voice and automations can still ask for one part. "Print spelling practice for Rosie" prints the pack. "Print a spelling sheet", "print a spelling word search" and "print a spelling crossword" print just that part, as do `type: spelling`, `spelling_search` and `spelling_crossword` in the actions. Re-import the Assist blueprint to get the new phrases.

## Fixed
- **The spelling crossword no longer waits on the AI at print time.** With an AI task set up, Tiny Press now writes the clues ahead of time: once at startup, and about 10 seconds after a kid's spelling to-do list changes (a word added or checked off). Only words without a clue are sent, so printing normally starts straight away.
  - A word whose AI clue fails the checks prints as a scramble and isn't asked about again at print time; the background writer tries it again the next time the list changes.
  - If clues aren't ready yet (a brand-new word, or Home Assistant just started), a print waits at most 20 seconds per request for the AI, down from 45, before using scrambles.
- **The card says what it's doing.** While a print is being made, the card shows "Getting the spelling crossword ready…" instead of "Working on it…". Tiles stay greyed out until it's done, so a tap during that time doesn't print. Before, a word search tapped during the crossword's wait was dropped without a word, and the cooldown that started after the crossword then blocked it.
- **Print counts last across restarts and upgrades.** Kid-mode counts (and paper used, the last print and answer keys) are now written within about 2 seconds of a print, instead of 10, so even an unclean stop keeps them. Home Assistant also writes anything pending when it shuts down. A future change to the storage format will carry the saved data over instead of starting fresh. Today's counts from before per-kid limits are kept too: v0.11.0 used to drop them on the day you upgraded.

## Added
- **Progress on the tile you pressed.** It fills in green while the print is being made, then flashes full when it's done. Bedtime stories and next chapters take the longest. The fill is paced by how long that kind of print took last time on this device, starting from a rough guess (about 45 seconds for a story), so it fills more accurately after the first print. It's an estimate: Home Assistant doesn't report real progress while the AI writes.
