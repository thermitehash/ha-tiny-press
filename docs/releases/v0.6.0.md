# v0.6.0 — Morning slip

A short printout to start the day: date, weather, today's events, to-dos and something fun.

## Added
- **Morning slip** (`tiny_press.print_morning_slip`, the **Morning slip** button, a card button, or `print` with `type: morning_slip`). It prints:
  - "Good morning!" and today's date.
  - **Weather:** an icon, the temperature now, today's high and low, and the chance of rain, from the weather entity you chose. The section is skipped if there's no weather entity.
  - **Today:** events from the calendars you pick, all-day events first, then by time. Events still going from yesterday show as "Ongoing". Duplicates across calendars print once, and a busy day ends with "+N more".
  - **To-dos (optional, off by default):** unchecked items from the to-do lists you pick, as checkboxes.
  - **At the bottom**, your choice in Configure → Settings: a joke (punchline upside down), a fun fact (with its source), a small 6×6 sudoku at the adult profile's difficulty (with an answer key), or nothing.
- **Generic calendars and to-dos:** any calendar or to-do integration works, so Skylight (once something exposes it) plugs in with no code changes.
- **Manual only:** no schedules or automations anywhere.
- **Limits:** the morning slip never counts toward kid-mode limits. From a **kid-mode dashboard card** it's limited to one a day (the card sends `kid_mode: true`). Your own card, the button and actions are unlimited.
