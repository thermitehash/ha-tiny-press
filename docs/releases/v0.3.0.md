# v0.3.0 — Dashboard card and kid limits

A dashboard card made for little fingers, plus limits so a happy six-year-old can't print the whole roll.

## Added
- **Tiny Press dashboard card** (`custom:tiny-press-card`). It ships inside the integration and loads automatically, so there's no separate HACS dashboard install. Add it from the card picker ("Tiny Press").
  - Big print buttons: Maze, Word search, Math, Joke, Fun fact, Kid pack. You choose which ones to show.
  - Status chips: online, paper (OK / low / out / cover open), metres of paper used, and prints left today.
  - Profile chips to switch the kid, and a maze difficulty toggle.
  - Last print with its `#ID`, and an **Answers** button.
  - **Preview before printing** (optional): see the exact black-and-white print, then Shuffle or Print. Print uses the same seed, so you get exactly what you saw.
  - **Kid mode:** hold-to-print (a short tap does nothing), no settings and no answers. It shows the countdown ("Next print in 1:23") and "That's enough printing for today!".
  - Works in light and dark themes, and is configured in the visual card editor.
- **Kid-mode limits** (Configure → Kid-mode limits; off by default):
  - A wait between prints, prints per hour, prints per day, and a daily paper budget.
  - Enforced by the integration, so buttons, cards, actions, voice and Siri share the same limits. Only kid prints count. Counts reset at local midnight.
  - Parents skip the limits with `ignore_limits: true` on `tiny_press.print`, or a "parent card" option. Admin users can skip them too if you turn that option on.
  - Blocked prints get a friendly message and fire `tiny_press_rate_limited`.
  - New sensor: **Kid prints left today**, with `next_print_at`, `paper_left_mm` and `blocked_reason` attributes.
- **Answer keys:** `tiny_press.print_answers` (newest puzzle, or a `print_id` from the footer) and an **Answers** button. Covers mazes (solution path), word searches (words marked), math strips (answers filled in) and jokes.
- **Kid sudoku:** 4×4 and 6×6 puzzles, each with exactly one solution.
  - Picture symbols (circle, square, triangle, star, heart, diamond) for kindergarten and grade 1; numbers for older kids.
  - Easy is 4×4, medium and hard are 6×6. Without a difficulty, the kid's grade picks the level.
  - The answer key shows the given cells bold and the answers lighter.
  - The generator already handles 9×9 for the adult puzzles in v0.4.0.
- **Spelling practice** from a kid's own list (for example this week's school words). Add the list in Configure → Edit a profile, or pass `words` to the action.
  - **Spelling practice** prints "Unscramble the words" and "Fill in the missing letters" (about a third of the letters blanked, never the first).
  - **Spelling word search** hides exactly those words, and every one is placed. The grid grows a little if needed.
  - Both have answer keys and count toward kid-mode limits.
- New buttons: Kid sudoku, Spelling practice, Spelling word search. New `print` types: `kid_sudoku`, `spelling`, `spelling_search`. New fields: `words` and `pictures`.
- **Weather print:** current conditions plus a forecast of up to 10 days.
  - Each day gets a drawn weather icon, its high and low, and the chance of rain or snow.
  - The data comes from the weather entity chosen in Configure → Settings, or from your only weather entity if you have just one.
  - It uses the daily forecast, falls back to twice-daily, and prints current conditions alone if the entity gives no forecast.
  - New **Weather** button, a `print` type `weather`, and a Weather button on the card.
- **`tiny_press.preview`:** returns the print as a PNG data URI plus its seed, without printing.

## Changed
- The phase plan moves up one minor version: adult puzzles are now v0.4.0, stories v0.5.0, the morning slip v0.6.0 and voice v0.7.0.
