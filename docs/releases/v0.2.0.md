# v0.2.0 — Kid pack

Word searches, math strips, jokes and fun facts, all tuned to a kid's reading grade. Plus the Kid pack: three puzzles in one print.

## Added
- **Profiles** (Settings → Tiny Press → Configure): add, edit and remove kids and adults.
  - A kid profile has a name, a reading grade (K–5, default 3), free-text interests, and an optional "stretch" toggle that makes puzzles one grade harder.
  - An adult profile has a name and a default puzzle difficulty (used from v0.3.0).
- **Kid profile select:** chooses who the buttons print for. Actions can also name a profile with `profile`.
- **Word search:** 10×10 up to grade 3 and 12×12 from grade 4. K–3 words come from the Dolch sight-word lists.
  - Words run across and down; diagonals are added from grade 3.
  - Words come from bundled grade lists plus words related to the kid's interests (24 themes, such as dinosaurs, space, horses and ocean).
  - Every listed word is guaranteed to be in the grid. The word list prints under the grid with tick boxes.
- **Math strip:** 10 problems and a harder bonus.
  - Grade 2: two-digit add/subtract where every problem needs regrouping.
  - Grade 3: adds ×/÷ facts to 10 and two word problems.
  - K–1 and grades 4–5 are covered too.
  - Multi-digit add/subtract is printed stacked, like a worksheet.
- **Joke and fun fact:** from bundled, human-written lists by default.
  - 111 fun facts, each taken from a U.S. government science page (NOAA Ocean Facts, NASA Science, National Park Service). These pages are public domain. Each fact prints with a small "Source:" credit.
  - 103 jokes.
  - Tiny Press doesn't repeat any of the last 40 printed. A joke's punchline prints upside down below a "fold here" line.
  - Optionally, switch on **Use AI for jokes and fun facts** to ask your `ai_task` entity first. The prompt has kid-safe rules and the output is strictly checked, with the bundled lists as the fallback.
- **Kid pack:** maze + word search + math strip in one job with one cut. Each puzzle has its own ID and answer key.
- New buttons: Word search, Math strip, Joke and Kid pack. New `tiny_press.print` types: `word_search`, `math`, `joke`, `fact` and `kid_pack`.
- Joke and fact responses include `setup`, `punchline` and `source` (`ai` or `offline`), so a Shortcut can read the joke aloud. Kid pack responses include `print_ids`.
- Options: an `ai_task` entity selector and a "Use AI for jokes and fun facts" switch (off by default).
- `data/LICENSE.md` documents every bundled data source and its license.

## Changed
- Diagnostics redact profile names and interests.
- Generators and data files now run in the executor.
