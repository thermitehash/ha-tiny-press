# v0.5.0 — Bedtime stories

Stories written for your kid's reading level, plus choose-your-own-adventure chapters that continue night after night.

## Added
- **`tiny_press.print_story`:** a bedtime story from your `ai_task` entity (for example the Anthropic integration).
  - **Fields:** `profile`, `topic`, `length` (short 250–350, medium 400–550, long 600–750 words) and `mode` (`standalone` or `serial`).
  - **Written for the kid:** pitched at the kid's reading grade, read aloud by a grown-up, using the kid's interests.
  - **Kid-safe rules in the prompt:** nothing frightening, kind endings, no real people, brands or copyrighted characters.
  - **No name sent:** the main character is always made up, and the kid's name is never sent to the AI.
  - **Length:** Tiny Press counts the words, asks once more if the story is far too short, and trims a long story at a paragraph break with a gentle closing line.
  - **Output checks:** links or markup in the AI's output mean the story isn't used.
- **Choose-your-own-adventure:** `mode: serial` starts a series, and each chapter ends with "What happens next? Circle one:" and choices A and B.
  - `tiny_press.continue_story` with `choice: A` or `B` continues it. With no choice, the AI picks the gentler option.
  - `final: true` gives a cozy last chapter.
  - Each kid's series (summary, chapter number, recent choices) is remembered.
- **Offline fallback:** with no `ai_task` entity, or if the AI fails, Tiny Press prints one of its bundled stories (two per length, written for Tiny Press) and explains why in a Home Assistant notification. An error never gets printed on paper.
  - A serial chapter that can't be written keeps the series for the next night.
  - Public-domain classics (Burgess, Potter, Aesop) are planned for when Project Gutenberg is reachable again.
- **Story length** select, plus **Bedtime story** and **Next chapter** buttons. `tiny_press.print` also takes `type: story`.
- **Responses** include `title`, `chapter`, `choices` and `source`, so a Shortcut can read out the choices.
- **Dashboard card:** Bedtime story and Next chapter buttons, plus Story length chips (hidden in kid mode).
- Stories print at normal, book-page size and count toward kid-mode limits.
