# v0.11.1 — Thanksgiving

## Added
- **Thanksgiving** is now a built-in day in the *Cultural and heritage days (US)* group, on the fourth Thursday of November. Like the rest of that group, it is off until you tick the group under **Holidays**, and it gets themed puzzles but no jokes.

## Fixed
- A holiday from a Holidays calendar that is also a built-in day (for example "Thanksgiving Day" or "Juneteenth National Independence Day") now prints one banner, the built-in one, instead of two. Other calendar holidays, such as "Day after Thanksgiving", still print as before.
