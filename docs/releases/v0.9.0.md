# v0.9.0 — Feeds and a live quote of the day

Print headlines from any RSS or Atom feed, and get Wikiquote's quote of the day on the morning slip.

## Added
- **Feeds** (Configure → Add a feed): give a feed a name, its URL and how many items to print (1–10). Tiny Press reads the feed once to check it, then fetches it only when you print. Nothing polls.
- **`tiny_press.print_feed`**: the newest items from a feed, each with its headline, date and a short summary.
  - Pictures, scripts and markup are dropped, and links are kept only if they are web addresses.
  - **`qr: true`** prints a small QR code under each item that opens the article on a phone. It's off by default, because it makes the print longer.
  - The `print` and `preview` actions also accept `type: feed`, which uses the first feed.
- **Dashboard card feed buttons:** add for example `feeds: [{name: News, feed: BBC News, items: 5, qr: true}]` to the card's settings. They're hidden in kid mode.
- **Morning slip "Headlines from a feed" bottom:** the three newest headlines from the feed picked under Settings → Morning slip feed (or the first feed). If the feed can't be read, a quote prints instead.
- **Live quote of the day:** the morning slip's Quote bottom now tries Wikiquote's quote of the day first, credited "Wikiquote (CC BY-SA 4.0)".
  - The quote is fetched once a day and cached, through the MediaWiki API's parse module. If the API can't be reached, Tiny Press reads the page directly.
  - If Wikiquote can't be reached, or today's quote is longer than 500 characters, a bundled public-domain quote prints instead.
  - Turn this off with Settings → Live quote of the day.
- **Voice and AI:**
  - New voice phrases "print the news" and "print the headlines from BBC News". Existing automations made from the blueprint need **Feed headlines** ticked under Phrases to use, after you re-import the blueprint.
  - AI agents can ask for `type: feed` with a feed's name.
  - `tiny_press.voice_print` accepts `type: feed`.

## Limits
- Feed prints are not kid prints, so they never count toward kid-mode limits.
- Downloads time out after 15 seconds and are capped at 2 MB. Feeds that declare XML entities are refused.

## Fixed
- `tiny_press.print` with `type: history` printed the test page. It now explains that history needs `tiny_press.print_history`.
