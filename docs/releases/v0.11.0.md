# v0.11.0 — Per-person card and limits, separate grades, kid names and per-kid mazes

## Added
- **Math grade for kids.** Kid profiles now have a **Math grade** next to the **Reading grade**, so a kid can read at grade 3 and do grade 1 math (Configure → Add a kid, or Edit a profile).
  - The reading grade sets word searches, spelling and story reading level.
  - The math grade sets math strips and kid sudoku.
  - **Stretch** (one grade harder) applies to both.
  - Profiles saved before this version use their reading grade for math until you change it.

- **Kid's name on their prints:** for example "Rosie's maze", "Rosie's word search", "Rosie's math", "Joke time, Rosie!". This covers mazes, word searches, math, kid sudoku, spelling, jokes and facts. Answer keys and stories keep their own titles.
- **Maze difficulty per kid** (Configure → Add a kid, or Edit a profile): Easy, Medium or Hard, or follow the Maze difficulty select (the default). Asking for a level ("print a hard maze") still wins. Kid packs use it too.
- **Profiles linked to people:** kid and adult profiles can be linked to a Home Assistant user. The card then follows who's logged in:
  - **A kid** gets kid mode with only their own prints left, time bar and story.
  - **An admin** gets a **Print as** row for every kid and adult, and never counts against limits.
  - **A linked adult** prints as themselves, never limited.
  - **Shared accounts** work as before.
- **Kid-mode limits per child:** each kid has their own cooldown, hourly and daily counts and paper budget. A print counts against the kid it's for. The prints-left sensor shows the active kid, and lists every kid under a `kids` attribute.
- **Enforced in the integration:**
  - A kid's own account always prints as that kid, and can't skip limits or print as someone else.
  - Admins and linked adults never count.
  - The kid-mode morning slip is once a day per kid.
- **Card time bar:** while a print has to wait, a live bar sits at the top of the card with the countdown inside it ("Next print in 0:44"). The sensor has a new `blocked_since` attribute (when the wait began).
- **Live Next chapter button:** the card's tile shows the kid's story ("Chapter 3 · The Lighthouse Fox"), or is greyed out with "No story yet". The button entity has `story`, `chapter`, `choices`, `profile` and every kid's story under `stories`.

## Changed
- **First-grade math strips** are single-digit addition and subtraction: both numbers from 0 to 9, and no negative answers. They used to be add and subtract within 20. The ★ bonus problem is a gentle step up: add or subtract within 20.
