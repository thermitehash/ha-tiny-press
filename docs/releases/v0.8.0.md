# v0.8.0 — Voice

Print by voice from Assist, and let AI agents print for you.

## Added
- **Voice phrases blueprint** (`blueprints/automation/tiny_press/voice_phrases.yaml`, with an Import button in the README). One automation covers every print:
  - Kid prints: "print a hard maze for Rosie", "print a word search / math sheet / kid pack / kid sudoku", "print spelling practice for Rosie".
  - "Tell me a joke" and "tell me a fun fact" print it and read it aloud.
  - Stories: "print a bedtime story", "print a story about a brave dragon", "print the next chapter".
  - Adult prints: "print an easy sudoku / crossword / cryptogram", "print a puzzle pack", "print the morning slip", "print the weather".
  - Notes: "print a note that says feed the cat".
  - Tiny Press always answers: what it printed, or why it didn't (kid-mode limits, printer offline, no weather entity, empty spelling list).
  - Every group's phrases are editable, and any group can be switched off. Phrases ship in English.
- **AI-agent tools.** When an Assist pipeline's AI agent (Claude, OpenAI, …) has Assist control, Tiny Press gives it **Print on Tiny Press** and **Print a note on Tiny Press**. Requests like "print my daughter a dinosaur story" work without "Prefer handling commands locally".
- **`tiny_press.voice_print`**, the action behind both. It returns `printed` and `speech` even when nothing printed, so voice requests never fail silently.

## Limits
- Kid prints by voice or AI count toward kid-mode limits, the same as the card and buttons.
- Notes are adult prints: never limited, capped at 500 characters.
- Adult puzzles, the morning slip and the weather are never limited.

## Changed
- Siri and iOS Shortcuts are no longer planned.
- RSS/Atom feed prints, a live Wikiquote quote of the day and a feed bottom for the morning slip are planned for v0.9.0.
