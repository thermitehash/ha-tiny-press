# v0.7.0 — Sensor history

Print the history of any sensor over a time period, as a graph, a table, or both.

## Added
- **`tiny_press.print_history`** for one to three entities.
  - **Period:** the last `hours` (default 24) or `days`, or a specific `start` and `end`.
  - **Numeric sensors** (temperature, humidity, power, …) print as a line graph with dotted gridlines and time labels. Sensors that share a unit share a graph and are told apart by line style (solid, dashed, dotted), with a legend. Under the graph comes a summary: now, min, max, time-weighted average and change.
  - **On/off and text sensors** (doors, motion, presence, …) print as a timeline: one lane per state with light hatching, so it doesn't slow the printer the way solid black does. Totals follow, such as "open: 42 min (3x)".
  - **Style:** `graph` (graph and summary, the default), `table` (summary plus a ruled table of hourly or daily min/average/max, or of state changes), or `both`.
  - **`tall: true`** draws the graph long and turns it onto the strip. Turn the receipt sideways and it reads like a normal landscape page: the header at the far left (the bottom of the strip), time labels running with the data, and every label the same way up.
  - **`grid: true`** adds time gridlines and a frame.
  - **Tables** are ruled, with a header row and column and row divider lines.
  - **Long periods:** over 3 days, Tiny Press uses the recorder's hourly long-term statistics when they exist, which also draws a thin min/max band.
  - **Missing data:** entities with no history for the period say so on the print instead of failing.
- **Dashboard card history buttons:** in the card's settings, add a list such as `- {name: Freezer, entity: sensor.freezer, hours: 24}`. Each entry also accepts `entities`, `days`, `style`, `tall`, `grid`, `title` and `icon`. The buttons are hidden in kid mode.
- **Not limited:** history prints never count toward kid-mode limits.
